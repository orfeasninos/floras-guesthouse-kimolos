Flora's Guest House is a bright 48 m² apartment on the first floor of a traditional house in Chorio, the whitewashed capital of Kimolos. Thick Cycladic walls keep it cool through the summer heat, and the private balcony opens onto quiet lanes where life still moves at island pace.

Inside there is a bedroom with two beds for up to three guests, a full kitchen for slow breakfasts with local produce, a bathroom with shower, two air-conditioning units and fast Wi-Fi. Everything else — the tavernas, the cafés, the village square — is a short stroll away.

Location

Exploring Kimolos from Chorio

Kimolos is a small, unhurried island just across from Milos, reached by ferry to Psathi port or the short hop from Pollonia. From the house you can walk everywhere in Chorio, and the island's beaches are a short drive or a scenic hike away.
